What to expect

Four simple steps to a healthier smile

From your first phone call to your personalized plan, we make getting started stress-free.

Step 1 Book your visit

Request an appointment online or by phone. We'll confirm a time that fits your schedule.

Step 2 Complete your forms

Arrive 10–15 minutes early (or ask us to send forms ahead) to fill out your new-patient paperwork.

Step 3 Meet & get examined

We'll review your history, take any needed X-rays, and give you a gentle, thorough exam.

Step 4 Plan your care

Dr. Gampa explains your options in plain language, and together we build a comfortable plan.

FAQ

New patient questions

Have a question we didn't cover? Call us at (937) 294-9400 — we're happy to help.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring a photo ID, your dental insurance card (if you have one), a list of any medications you take, and details of any dental concerns. Arriving 10–15 minutes early lets you complete your new-patient paperwork comfortably.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans and will file claims on your behalf. Not sure if you're covered? Call us at (937) 294-9400 and we'll gladly verify your benefits before your visit.

What if I don't have insurance?

No problem at all. We offer flexible payment options and CareCredit financing so you can get the care you need on a budget that works for you. We'll always review costs with you in advance — no surprises.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely — many of our patients feel the same way. We go at your pace, explain everything clearly, and offer comfort and sedation options so you can relax. There's no judgment here, ever.
