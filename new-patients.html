New Patients

Your first visit, made easy

We can't wait to meet you. Here's everything you need to feel prepared and right at home from the start.

What to expect

Four simple steps to a healthier smile

01  Reserve your visit

Request an appointment online or by phone. We'll confirm a time that fits your schedule.

02  Complete your forms

Arrive 10–15 minutes early (or ask us to send forms ahead) to fill out new-patient paperwork.

03  Meet & get examined

We review your history, take any needed X-rays, and give a gentle, thorough exam.

04  Plan your care

Dr. Gampa explains your options in plain language, and together we build a comfortable plan.

Insurance & financing

Care that fits your budget

Great dental care shouldn't be out of reach. We work hard to make treatment affordable and transparent.

  • Most PPO insurance accepted — we file your claims for you.
  • CareCredit & flexible payment plans to spread out the cost of care.
  • Clear estimates up front — you'll always know costs before we begin.
  • We accept cash, credit & debit cards, HSA and FSA.

FAQ

New patient questions

Have a question we didn't cover? Call us at (937) 294-9400 — we're happy to help.

Book a visit
01What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring a photo ID, your dental insurance card (if you have one), a list of medications, and details of any concerns. Arriving 10–15 minutes early lets you complete paperwork comfortably.

02What insurance do you accept?

We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans and file claims on your behalf. Not sure if you're covered? Call (937) 294-9400 and we'll gladly verify your benefits.

03What if I don't have insurance?

No problem. We offer flexible payment options and CareCredit financing so you can get the care you need on a budget that works. We always review costs in advance — no surprises.

04I'm nervous about the dentist — can you help?

Absolutely; many of our patients feel the same. We go at your pace, explain everything, and offer comfort and sedation options. There's no judgment here, ever.
