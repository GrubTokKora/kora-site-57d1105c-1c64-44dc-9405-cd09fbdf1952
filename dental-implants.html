Dental Implants

Dental implants that look and feel real

Missing teeth affect more than your smile — they change how you eat, speak and feel. Dental implants replace the whole tooth, root and all, giving you a result that looks, functions and lasts like the real thing.

Dr. Gampa evaluates your bone and gum health, plans treatment with precise imaging, and guides you comfortably through every step.

  • A permanent solution — implants can last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Protects your jawbone — implants stimulate bone and prevent a sunken look.
  • Eat and smile freely — no slipping, no restrictions — just natural function.

01Am I a candidate for implants?

Most healthy adults with adequate bone are good candidates. Even with bone loss, grafting or mini implants may work. A quick consult and imaging give a clear answer.

02Does getting an implant hurt?

The procedure is done with thorough numbing and gentle technique — most patients are surprised how comfortable it is. Any soreness afterward is usually mild.

03How long does the process take?

It varies. After placement the implant typically needs a few months to fuse with the bone before the final tooth is attached. We'll give you a personalized timeline.

04How do I care for implants?

Just like natural teeth — brush, floss and keep up with checkups. With good hygiene, implants are one of the most durable investments in your smile.
