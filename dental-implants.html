FAQ

Questions about dental implants

Still curious? Call us at (937) 294-9400 and we'll gladly help.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with adequate bone are good candidates. Even if you've lost bone, options like grafting or mini implants may still work. A quick consultation and imaging will give you a clear answer.

Does getting an implant hurt?

The procedure is done with thorough numbing and gentle technique, and most patients are surprised by how comfortable it is. Any post-op soreness is usually mild and easily managed.

How long does the implant process take?

It varies. After placement, the implant typically needs a few months to fuse with the bone before the final tooth is attached. We'll give you a personalized timeline at your consultation.

How do I care for implants?

Just like natural teeth — brush, floss and keep up with regular checkups. With good hygiene, implants are one of the most durable investments you can make in your smile.
