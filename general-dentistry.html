General & preventive dentistry

Preventive care is the foundation of a healthy mouth — and the easiest way to avoid bigger problems down the road. At Spring Dental, gentle cleanings and thorough exams help you keep your natural teeth for life.

When treatment is needed, we restore teeth conservatively using natural-looking, tooth-colored materials, and we always take the time to explain your options first.

FAQ

Questions about general dentistry

Still curious? Call us at (937) 294-9400 and we'll gladly help.

How often should I have a dental cleaning?

For most people, a checkup and cleaning every six months is ideal. If you're prone to gum disease or have other risk factors, we may recommend more frequent visits to keep your smile healthy.

Are digital X-rays safe?

Yes. Digital X-rays use up to 80% less radiation than traditional film and give us instant, detailed images to diagnose problems accurately. We only take X-rays when they're needed for your care.

Do you offer tooth-colored fillings?

Absolutely. We use durable, mercury-free composite fillings that are matched to your natural tooth color, so repairs are virtually invisible.

What if I haven't been to the dentist in a while?

That's completely okay — many of our patients come to us after a long gap, and there's no judgment here. We'll start with a gentle exam and build a comfortable plan to get your smile back on track.
