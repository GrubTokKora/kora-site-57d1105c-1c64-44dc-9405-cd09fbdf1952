FAQ

Questions about oral surgery

Still curious? Call us at (937) 294-9400 and we'll gladly help.

Will my procedure hurt?

We use thorough numbing and, when appropriate, sedation so you stay comfortable throughout. Most patients feel only mild soreness afterward, which is easily managed with the aftercare we provide.

Do you offer sedation for anxious patients?

Yes. We offer anxiolysis and other comfort options to help nervous patients relax. Let us know your concerns and we'll tailor your visit so you feel at ease.

When should wisdom teeth be removed?

Wisdom teeth are often removed in the late teens or twenties when they're impacted, crowding other teeth, or hard to keep clean. We'll take an X-ray and let you know if and when removal is recommended.

What is recovery like after an extraction?

Most people recover in a few days. We'll give you detailed post-op instructions — rest, soft foods and avoiding straws early on — to help you heal quickly and comfortably.
