FAQ

Questions about emergency dentistry

Still curious? Call us at (937) 294-9400 and we'll gladly help.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe or persistent toothache, swelling, bleeding, a broken or knocked-out tooth, or a lost filling or crown are all reasons to call us right away. When in doubt, phone us and we'll help you decide.

What should I do if my tooth gets knocked out?

Handle it by the crown (not the root), gently rinse it, and try to place it back in the socket or keep it in milk. Then call us immediately — acting within the first hour gives the best chance of saving the tooth.

How do I manage pain until my appointment?

Rinse with warm salt water, use a cold compress for swelling, and take an over-the-counter pain reliever as directed. Avoid placing aspirin directly on the gum. Call us and we'll get you in as soon as possible.

Do you see emergencies for patients who aren't established here?

Yes — you don't have to be an existing patient. Call us and we'll do our best to see you the same day and get you comfortable.
