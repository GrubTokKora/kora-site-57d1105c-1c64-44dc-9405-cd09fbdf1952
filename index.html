FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most. Have another? Just ask — we're happy to help.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — we welcome new patients of all ages and would love to meet your family. You can request a visit online or call us at (937) 294-9400, and we'll get you scheduled quickly.

Do you take my dental insurance?

We work with most major PPO dental insurance plans and file claims on your behalf. For patients without insurance or needing help spreading out costs, we offer flexible payment options and CareCredit financing. Call us and we'll gladly verify your benefits.

Can you see me today for a dental emergency?

We reserve time each day for urgent problems like toothaches, broken or knocked-out teeth, and lost fillings or crowns. Call us first thing and we'll do everything we can to see you the same day and get you out of pain.

Where are you located?

We're at 5515 N Springboro Pike in Dayton, OH 45449, conveniently serving West Carrollton, Kettering, Centerville, Miamisburg and the surrounding Miami Valley communities. Free parking is available at the door.
