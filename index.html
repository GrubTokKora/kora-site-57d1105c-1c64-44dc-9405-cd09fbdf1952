About Our Practice

Dentistry built around
how you actually feel

At Spring Dental, we take the time to listen before we ever pick up a tool. Led by Dr. Gampa, our team combines clinical precision with a genuinely warm chair-side manner — so whether you're in for a routine clean or something more involved, you'll always know exactly what's happening and why.

We're proud to serve families across Dayton and the surrounding area from our convenient location on N Springboro Pike. New patients are always welcome — call us and we'll get you in quickly.
