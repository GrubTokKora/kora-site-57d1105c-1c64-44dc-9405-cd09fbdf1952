Meet your dentist

Dr. Ravinder Gampa, DDS

Dr. Ravinder Gampa received his Bachelor of Dental Surgery in 1982 and his Master's in Oral Medicine & Radiology in 1987 from Osmania University in Hyderabad, India. He served as an Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Oral Medicine & Radiology at Osmania for several years before moving to New Zealand in 1996.

Since 2000, Dr. Gampa has practiced general dentistry at our West Carrollton location and been an active member of the Dayton dental community. He takes pride in offering comprehensive, compassionate care to every patient, and regularly attends continuing education to stay current with advances in dentistry.

Welcome to the compassionate zone

A dental home, not just a dental office

The moment you walk in, you'll notice a friendly, compassionate team and a welcoming smile that puts you at ease. We want you to feel that this is your dental home as much as it is ours.

Formerly West Carrollton Family Dental, Spring Dental has proudly served West Carrollton, Dayton, Kettering, Miamisburg, Centerville and Oakwood for over fifteen years. We promise to do our best to meet your expectations and keep your gums and teeth healthy — so you can enjoy your smile for years to come.
