Meet your dentist

Dr. Ravinder Gampa, DDS

Dr. Ravinder Gampa received his Bachelor of Dental Surgery in 1982 and his Master's in Oral Medicine & Radiology in 1987 from Osmania University in Hyderabad, India. Following his education, he served as an Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Oral Medicine & Radiology at Osmania University for several years before moving to New Zealand in 1996.

After a brief period in New Zealand, Dr. Gampa began caring for patients at our West Carrollton location. Since 2000, he has practiced general dentistry and been an active member of the Dayton dental community. He takes pride in offering comprehensive, compassionate care to every patient, and regularly attends continuing education to stay current with advances in dentistry.

Welcome to the compassionate zone

A dental home, not just a dental office

The moment you walk in, you'll notice a friendly, compassionate team and a welcoming smile that puts you at ease. We want you to feel that this is your dental home as much as it is ours.

Formerly known as West Carrollton Family Dental, Spring Dental has proudly served West Carrollton, Dayton, Kettering, Miamisburg, Centerville and Oakwood for over 15 years, with a location serving Springfield as well. We promise to do our best to meet your expectations and keep your gums and teeth healthy — so you can enjoy your smile for years to come.
