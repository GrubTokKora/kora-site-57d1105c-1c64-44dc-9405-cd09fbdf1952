FAQ

Questions about orthodontics

Still curious? Call us at (937) 294-9400 and we'll gladly help.

How long does Invisalign take?

Most treatments take about 6–18 months depending on how much movement is needed. We'll map out your plan and give you a clear estimate before you begin.

Is Invisalign more expensive than braces?

Costs are often comparable, and many insurance plans that cover orthodontics also cover Invisalign. We offer financing to make treatment affordable and will review the numbers with you upfront.

Are clear aligners comfortable?

Yes — aligners are smooth and custom-fit. You may feel gentle pressure for a day or two when switching to a new tray, which is a sign your treatment is working.

Can adults get orthodontic treatment?

Definitely. A large share of orthodontic patients today are adults. Clear aligners and tooth-colored braces make it easy to straighten your teeth discreetly at any age.
