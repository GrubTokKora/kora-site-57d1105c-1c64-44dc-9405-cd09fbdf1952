Orthodontics & Invisalign

Straighter teeth, discreetly

Straight teeth aren't just about looks — aligned teeth are easier to clean and better for long-term health. Our discreet options make it easier than ever to get the smile you want.

With Invisalign clear aligners, you straighten your teeth using a series of comfortable, nearly invisible trays you can remove to eat, brush and floss.

  • Nearly invisible — aligners are clear and removable — most people won't notice.
  • Comfortable & convenient — no wires to poke, and you eat whatever you like.
  • Healthier long-term — straight teeth are easier to keep clean and cavity-free.

01How long does Invisalign take?

Most treatments take about 6–18 months depending on how much movement is needed. We'll map out your plan and give a clear estimate first.

02Is Invisalign more expensive than braces?

Costs are often comparable, and many plans that cover orthodontics also cover Invisalign. We offer financing and review the numbers upfront.

03Are clear aligners comfortable?

Yes — they're smooth and custom-fit. You may feel gentle pressure for a day or two with each new tray, a sign the treatment is working.

04Can adults get treatment?

Definitely — a large share of orthodontic patients today are adults. Clear aligners and tooth-colored braces make it easy to straighten discreetly at any age.
