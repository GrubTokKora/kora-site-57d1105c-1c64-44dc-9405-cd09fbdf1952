FAQ

Questions about cosmetic dentistry

Still curious? Call us at (937) 294-9400 and we'll gladly help.

How white will my teeth get?

Results vary by person, but most patients see several shades of improvement. In-office whitening delivers the fastest, most dramatic results, and custom take-home trays let you maintain and touch up your smile.

Are veneers permanent?

Veneers are a long-term investment — with good care they can last 10–15 years or more. Because a small amount of enamel is prepared, the process is considered permanent, so we'll plan your smile carefully together.

What's the difference between bonding and veneers?

Bonding uses tooth-colored resin applied in a single visit and is ideal for small fixes, while veneers are custom porcelain shells that offer the most dramatic, durable results. We'll help you choose the right option.

Can I get a crown in one day?

Yes. With CEREC technology we can design, craft and place a natural-looking ceramic crown in a single visit — no messy impressions or temporary crowns required.
